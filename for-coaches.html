ONE SHARED PLAN · THREE CLEAR ROLES

Keep a good lesson working after practice ends.

Matwork organizes the handoff from a private wrestling lesson to purposeful practice, with useful information for the athlete, parent, and coach.

Open the shared workspace →
ATHLETE

Know what to practice

See coach assigned steps, check off practice, and tell your support team how the work is going.

PARENT

Support the plan

COACH

Teach, then follow through

Record the key lesson cues and practice assignments. Review shared practice progress before the next session.

THE CORE FLOW

Lesson → practice → next lesson

  1. Coach or parent records what was covered, keeping each person’s perspective clearly labeled.
  2. The athlete gets a short, practical checklist. The parent gets the fuller lesson context.
  3. The coach reviews completed work and feedback deliberately shared by the family.

Lesson history stays attached to the athlete and shows who recorded each session, giving multiple assigned coaches context to build from.

Private by design.
Each role has a separate view of the shared workspace. Parents can see the full family record; athletes see their own tasks and lessons; coaches see athletes assigned to them and feedback shared with them. Use first names only and avoid identifying details about young athletes.
