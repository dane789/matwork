A SIMPLE LOOP AFTER EVERY PRIVATE

Teach it. Reinforce it. Build on it.

A short handoff keeps the wrestler, family, and coach working from the same lesson.

01 · AFTER THE LESSON

Capture what matters

Jot down what you covered and what the athlete should practice. Type or dictate on your phone. Matwork organizes your notes into a clear lesson record and suggested next steps for you to review before sharing.

02 · BETWEEN LESSONS

The athlete knows the next step

Your assigned practice becomes a focused checklist. The athlete can mark work complete, so the plan stays visible between sessions.

03 · NEXT SESSION

Start with useful context

See checklist progress and family feedback they chose to share. Each lesson shows the coach’s name and what was covered, so another coach assigned to the athlete can review it before their session.

WHY COACHES USE MATWORK

Keep your attention on coaching.

Matwork gives your instruction a clear path from the mat to the athlete’s next practice, without asking you to write a long report after every session.

Less follow-up admin

Capture the essentials in your own words. Dictate on your phone or enter a short note; Matwork shapes it into a clear recap and suggested practice steps for you to review.

A more professional handoff

The athlete gets practical next steps. Parents see lesson context and can support the plan without having to interpret a scattered message.

Lesson history for the coaching team

Each lesson shows who taught it and what was covered, so another coach assigned to the athlete can review the history before their session.

ONE WORKSPACE, CLEAR PERMISSIONS

Everyone has a useful role.

ATHLETE

Practice the assigned steps

See your lessons and checklist. Mark work as practiced and share a brief check-in.

PARENT

Support and share perspective

See the fuller family record, add parent observations, and choose what feedback to share with the coach.

COACH

Teach and follow through

See athletes assigned to you, your lesson history, checklist progress, and family feedback shared with you.
