ONE SHARED PLAN · THREE CLEAR ROLES

Keep a good lesson working after practice ends.

Matwork organizes the handoff from a private wrestling lesson to purposeful practice, with useful information for the athlete, parent, and coach.

THE CORE FLOW

Lesson → practice → next lesson

  1. Coach notes or parent observations become a clear lesson record, with each person’s perspective labeled.
  2. The athlete gets a short checklist to put the lesson into action. Parents see the fuller context so they can reinforce the coach’s cues.
  3. The coach reviews completed practice and family feedback chosen to share, then uses it to shape the next session.
More time coaching. Less time organizing.

Matwork keeps lesson notes, practice follow-through, and family feedback together. Coaches can see what the athlete worked on before the next private, spend less time sorting updates, and focus on teaching. Each session names the coach who recorded it, so other assigned coaches can build on earlier instruction instead of starting from scratch.

Private by design.
Each role has a separate view of the shared workspace. Parents can see the full family record; athletes see their own tasks and lessons; coaches see athletes assigned to them and feedback shared with them. Use first names only and avoid identifying details about young athletes.
