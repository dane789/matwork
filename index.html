THE CORE SERVICE

One plan. Clear roles.
Steady progress.

Matwork focuses on the work that happens around a private lesson.

01 · COACHCapture what you taught

Save the lesson focus, the coach’s instructions, and the practice steps you want the athlete to carry forward.

02 · ATHLETE + PARENTPractice with a clear purpose

The athlete sees a simple checklist. Parents see the fuller lesson context and can support the plan.

03 · COACHUse the follow-through

Review completed practice and any family feedback shared with you to shape the next lesson.

Each lesson includes the coach’s name and what was covered. Another coach assigned to that athlete can review it before their session.

Private workspace, focused on the people involved.
Matwork’s shared area uses role-based access for the athlete, parent, and assigned coach. Keep identifying details about young athletes out of notes; first names are enough.
